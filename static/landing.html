About this project

The Zamboanga City poverty mapping initiative is a collaboration between local government, researchers, and development partners. It brings together satellite imagery, machine learning models, and official statistics to provide a more granular picture of poverty and vulnerability across the city.

Model predictions use grid-level estimates from CatBoost, Random Forest, and CNN models.

Census-based indicators help validate and interpret model results at the barangay level.

The platform is designed to support planning, targeting of social programs, and field verification.

Data & methods

  • Gridded poverty predictions derived from machine learning models trained on survey data.
  • Barangay-level indicators from official local and national statistics.
  • Geospatial processing and visualization powered by open-source tools.
